# Insight AI
**Status:** draft | **Hook:** Turn fragmented school data into decisions — without a single spreadsheet.

## 1. Foundation Hypothesis
**Universal Problem:** Student data across attendance, achievement, course access, and discipline is fragmented and dense, forcing educators to manually manipulate static spreadsheets with no ability to surface trends or collaborate on interventions.

### Segment 1: NYC Public School educators — principals, teachers, counselo
**Who:** NYC Public School educators — principals, teachers, counselors, and district staff
**Pain Point:** Student data across attendance, achievement, course access, and discipline is fragmented and dense, forcing educators to manually manipulate static spreadsheets with no ability to surface trends or collaborate on interventions.
**Resolution:** An intelligent data analytics platform that translates raw student metrics into intuitive visual narratives, contextual benchmarks, and shareable reports — enabling educators to surface key trends, annotate findings, and align on collaborative interventions.

**Universal Solution:** An intelligent data analytics platform that translates raw student metrics into intuitive visual narratives, contextual benchmarks, and shareable reports — enabling educators to surface key trends, annotate findings, and align on collaborative interventions.

## 2. Unfair Advantage
**Capability:** Real-time aggregation and visualization of multi-source student data (attendance, achievement, course access, discipline) in a single platform with AI-driven trend surfacing and annotation tools
**Motivation:** Educators spend hours manually cross-referencing spreadsheets and still miss the patterns that matter — they need answers, not more data
**Insight:** The schools with the best student outcomes do not have more data than others — they have faster, shared visibility into what the data means

## 3. Principles
- Clarity over completeness — show the signal, not the noise
- Context beats raw numbers — every metric needs a benchmark
- Shared visibility drives shared accountability
- Annotations are decisions — make them first-class
- Boring reliability > flashy features
- Outcome > Output

## 4. The Click Test
**Riskiest Assumption:** Principals and teachers will trust AI-surfaced trends enough to act on them without manually verifying the underlying spreadsheet data
**Test Method:** Show 10 NYC school staff a dashboard with 3 pre-loaded trend alerts and ask them to decide on an intervention — measure how many reference source data vs. act directly from the visual
**Success Metric:** 70%+ of participants take or discuss an intervention action within 5 minutes without opening a spreadsheet
