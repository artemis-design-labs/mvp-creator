# UX Case Study Generator
**Status:** hypothesis-set | **Hook:** Go from idea to a full UX case study in one prompt, not one month.

## 1. Foundation Hypothesis
**Universal Problem:** Building a full UX process — personas, journey maps, empathy maps, flows, wireframes — takes weeks and requires coordination across researchers, designers, and PMs. Most teams skip artifacts entirely or produce low-quality ones under pressure, leaving product decisions undocumented and hard to defend.

### Segment 1: Junior-to-mid UX designers and product managers at startups 
**Who:** Junior-to-mid UX designers and product managers at startups or agencies who need to run a UX process quickly but lack the time, team, or experience to produce all the artifacts from scratch.
**Pain Point:** Building a full UX process — personas, journey maps, empathy maps, flows, wireframes — takes weeks and requires coordination across researchers, designers, and PMs. Most teams skip artifacts entirely or produce low-quality ones under pressure, leaving product decisions undocumented and hard to defend.
**Resolution:** A web app that takes a pain point, idea, or brief as input and generates a complete, editable UX artifact suite in minutes: personas, journey maps, empathy maps, affinity diagrams, user flows, lo-fi wireframes, hi-fi prototypes, competitive analysis, usability metrics, and interview questions — all structured as a shareable case study.

**Universal Solution:** A web app that takes a pain point, idea, or brief as input and generates a complete, editable UX artifact suite in minutes: personas, journey maps, empathy maps, affinity diagrams, user flows, lo-fi wireframes, hi-fi prototypes, competitive analysis, usability metrics, and interview questions — all structured as a shareable case study.
**Anti-Customer:** Senior UX researchers who need primary research rigor, or enterprise teams with established design ops and dedicated research functions.

## 2. Unfair Advantage
**Capability:** Deep integration with Claude API for structured artifact generation, combined with Figma API for wireframe and prototype scaffolding — all orchestrated in a single workflow.
**Motivation:** We believe the best design decisions are documented ones. Most teams can't afford to do it properly — we want to make the starting framework free and instant.
**Insight:** Designers don't skip UX artifacts because they don't value them — they skip them because producing them is the bottleneck. AI removes that bottleneck entirely.

## 3. Principles
- Starting framework > polished deliverable (ship a useful draft, not a perfect artifact)
- Editable > static (every output must be modifiable by the team)
- Contextual > generic (artifacts must feel specific to the input, not templated)
- One input > full suite (minimize friction from idea to all artifacts)
- Case study ready > internal only (outputs should be presentable to a product team as-is)

## 4. The Click Test
**Riskiest Assumption:** Designers will trust AI-generated artifacts as a legitimate starting point rather than dismissing them as too generic to be useful.
**Test Method:** Build a single-artifact MVP (AI-generated persona only), share with 10 UX designers/PMs, and measure whether they edit and use it vs. discard it.
**Success Metric:** 7 of 10 testers keep and edit the generated artifact rather than starting from scratch.
