# Behavioral Analytics
**Status:** hypothesis-set | **Hook:** Stop guessing why users leave. Get research-grade UX insights from your existing traffic data — automatically.

## 1. Foundation Hypothesis
**Universal Problem:** Traditional web analytics tools (Google Analytics, Mixpanel) tell teams WHAT users do — page views, clicks, funnels — but not WHY they do it. Teams are left guessing at root causes, running expensive user interviews, and shipping fixes that miss the mark. The user's mental model is a blur to these product teams and they rely on assumptions to make product changes.

The common theme across all these customer segments is that they rely on quantitative data that cannot explain the reasoning behind recurring user behaviors like churn, drop-offs, low engagement time, minimal scroll depth, and lack of any kind of interaction.
**Pain Point:** Traditional web analytics tools (Google Analytics, Mixpanel) tell teams WHAT users do — page views, clicks, funnels — but not WHY they do it. **Anti-Customer:** Early-stage startups with <1,000 monthly visitors (insufficient data), and enterprise companies with dedicated in-house research teams and existing qualitative tooling.

## 2. Unfair Advantage
**Capability:** We can bridge quantitative event data with LLM-based synthesis to generate living, continuously-updated UX artifacts at a fraction of the cost of manual research. Since the data is consistently incoming, we do not have to hunt down users to conduct research.
**Motivation:** We believe most product teams are flying blind because the gap between analytics and research is too wide and too expensive to close manually. Product teams are at the mercy of standard analytic tools that do not tell a story about their user traffic that will help them understand how their products are perceived.
**Insight:** Teams do not lack data — they lack interpretation. The same behavioral signals that analytics tools discard (scroll depth, rage clicks, hesitation patterns, exit timing) contain the WHY that researchers spend weeks uncovering.

## 3. Principles
- WHY > WHAT — always surface intent, not just events
- Boring > Exciting — accurate, reliable artifacts beat flashy dashboards
- Living artifacts — everything updates as traffic changes, never goes stale
- Research-grade without research budgets
