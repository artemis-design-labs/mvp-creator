# Pulse
**Status:** active | **Hook:** Get your prototype in front of 100 matched users in 24 hours — plus AI agents that behave like your actual target customers, giving you feedback around the clock.

## 1. Foundation Hypothesis
**Universal Problem:** Founders ship products based on assumptions and biased feedback from friends. Traditional user research is slow, expensive, and hard to recruit for. Most products fail not because they are badly built, but because they solve the wrong problem for the wrong user or the problem is too small which in turns questions the scalability of the product.

## 2. Unfair Advantage
**Capability:** A dual validation engine: real human feedback from a persona-matched community, plus AI agents that simulate authentic user behavior based on the founder's own persona definitions — providing 24/7 feedback without recruitment lag.
**Motivation:** Founders pour years and capital into products that never find their market. The best time to catch a wrong assumption is before you scale, not after. 
**Insight:** Validation fails not because founders do not want feedback, but because getting it from the right people at the right moment is nearly impossible today. AI agents that behave like real users — not just answer surveys — change the economics of that entirely.

## 3. Principles
- Signal over noise — one validated insight from the right user beats 1,000 generic opinions
- Behavior over stated preference — watch what users do, not just what they say
- Matched friction — feedback must come from people who actually resemble the target persona
- Agents that behave, not just respond — AI simulation must mirror realistic user journeys
- Founder-first UX — the platform itself must be the simplest thing a founder has ever used

## 4. The Click Test
**Riskiest Assumption:** That AI agent feedback will be trusted and acted on by founders at the same level as real human feedback — and that the behavioral simulation is realistic enough to surface meaningful product insights.
**Test Method:** Run a closed beta with 30 early-stage founders. Each gets 5 real user sessions and 5 AI agent sessions for their prototype. After 2 weeks, run a blind survey asking founders to rate the quality and usefulness of feedback without telling them which was AI vs human.
**Success Metric:** 65%+ of founders rate AI agent feedback as as useful or more useful than real user feedback in at least one dimension (UX clarity, feature prioritization, or pain-point identification). Secondary: 80%+ retention after first validation run.
