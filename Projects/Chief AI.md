# Chief AI
**Status:** hypothesis-set | **Hook:** The lateral work your Chief of Staff would catch before it became a fire — tracked, owned, and chased automatically.

## 1. Foundation Hypothesis
**Universal Problem:** Every organization runs a hidden layer of critical-but-not-urgent work that falls between the cracks of every project management tool: security training completion for all employees, contract renewals, software licensing renewals and training modules, budget submissions, audit prep, policy sign-offs, onboarding checklists. This work has hard deadlines and real consequences when missed — legal exposure, audit failures, security vulnerabilities, license lapses. But the primary responsibility is with the chief of staff, so it gets tracked in a fragile mix of spreadsheets and calendar reminders. The person responsible is already at capacity. Nobody finds out a deadline slipped until it already has.

### Segment 1: Chiefs of Staff, Operations leads, and COOs at mid-sized com
**Who:** Chiefs of Staff, Operations leads, and COOs at mid-sized companies (50–500 employees) who are personally responsible for making sure cross-functional, deadline-driven operational work gets done — but are currently tracking it across spreadsheets, email threads, shared calendars, and manual follow-ups and a ton of slack follow=ups. Also relevant: any department head who owns compliance and renewal deadlines that sit outside their core job description

**Resolution:** An AI Chief of Staff — a web-based bot that acts as a persistent, proactive coordinator for all lateral, deadline-driven operational work. It ingests operational commitments (contracts, renewals, compliance deadlines, training requirements), assigns ownership to specific people, sends targeted reminders, escalates automatically as deadlines approach, and maintains a live dashboard of what is on track, at risk, and overdue. Unlike project management tools, it understands operational context — it knows security training affects all 200 employees individually and tracks per-person completion, not just the parent task. Unlike a human CoS, it never forgets, never sleeps, and never deprioritizes a renewal because a board meeting came up.

Teams looking for a general project management tool — this is not Asana for lateral work, it specifically excludes core job deliverables.

## 2. Unfair Advantage
**Capability:** The AI layer enables three things no checklist tool can do: (1) natural language intake — 'Add our AWS renewal, it's due March 15, owner is Sarah' creates a tracked item instantly; (2) context-aware escalation — the bot knows the difference between a 30-day warning on a $2k SaaS renewal and a 7-day warning on a $500k enterprise contract and escalates accordingly; (3) per-person completion tracking for population-level compliance work like security training, without requiring a separate HRIS integration.
**Motivation:** Lateral work failures are invisible until they are catastrophic. A lapsed contract, a missed audit, an employee who never completed security training — these do not show up in any OKR dashboard but they end careers and create legal liability. The CoS is the person who prevents this, and that job is currently done with highlighters and calendar blocks.
**Insight:** Every CoS tool on the market is either too simple (a shared to-do list) or too complex (a full ERP that requires a 6-month implementation). The real insight is that the Chief of Staff's job for lateral work is not project management — it is awareness, follow-through, and escalation. Those three functions, applied only to non-core deadline work, can be fully automated without trying to replace strategic CoS judgment.

## 3. Principles
- Lateral work only > Competing with Asana or Jira
- Escalate before the fire > Report after the fact
- Natural language in > Structured form entry
- Auto-chase > Manual reminder

## 4. The Click Test
**Riskiest Assumption:** Operations leads and Chiefs of Staff will trust an AI bot to autonomously send nudges and escalations on their behalf — without reviewing each one — because the time savings outweigh the loss of control over tone and timing.
**Test Method:** Run the AI CoS workflow manually for 2 paying design partner companies for 4 weeks. Use Claude to draft all nudges and escalations, send them from a shared inbox, and log every response and completion. This is a concierge MVP — no product built yet. Measure whether people complete lateral tasks faster than their baseline.
**Success Metric:** At least 1 of 2 pilot companies shows a measurable reduction in overdue lateral tasks (20%+ fewer missed deadlines vs. the prior 4-week period). At least 1 pilot requests to continue the engagement after the manual phase ends and asks when the product will be ready.
