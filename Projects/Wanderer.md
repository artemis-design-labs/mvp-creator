# Wanderer
**Status:** draft | **Hook:** Your trip does not stop when your plans fall apart — Wanderer re-routes you in seconds.

## 1. Foundation Hypothesis
**Universal Problem:** Travel is full of coordination overhead that current tools don't automate: tracking personal belongings across multiple stays, recovering from disruptions like cancellations and delays, understanding the true cost of activities, and getting personalized recommendations that match actual preferences — not generic lists.

### Segment 1: Frequent travelers (leisure and bleisure) who take multi-des
**Who:** Frequent travelers (leisure and bleisure) who take multi-destination trips and struggle to stay organized across locations, itineraries, and budgets.
**Pain Point:** Travel is full of coordination overhead that current tools don't automate: tracking personal belongings across multiple stays, recovering from disruptions like cancellations and delays, understanding the true cost of activities, and getting personalized recommendations that match actual preferences — not generic lists.
**Resolution:** An AI-powered mobile travel companion that manages luggage across locations, re-optimizes itineraries in real time when plans change, calculates true activity costs including transport, and surfaces personalized recommendations based on the user's own preference profile.

**Anti-Customer:** Casual vacationers who book a single resort and stay put, or budget backpackers whose only goal is to minimize spend.

## 2. Unfair Advantage
**Capability:** AI itinerary re-optimization, luggage item tracking across multi-stop trips, rideshare cost integration, and a personalized preference engine that improves with each trip.
**Motivation:** Travel should be experienced, not managed. The overhead of keeping track of things, costs, and contingencies pulls people out of the moment.
**Insight:** Existing travel apps (TripIt, Google Trips, Airbnb) solve discovery or booking — none solve real-time trip management after departure. The highest-value moment is when something goes wrong and the traveler needs instant re-planning.

## 3. Principles
- Real-time over pre-planned — the app earns its value during disruptions, not before the trip
- Accuracy over volume — one right recommendation beats ten generic ones
- Never make the user re-enter data — infer, sync, and remember
- Cost transparency is non-negotiable — always show the full picture
- The app factors real-time information that influences recommendations including real-time weather data.

## 4. The Click Test
**Riskiest Assumption:** Travelers will consistently log their personal items into the app across multiple locations — the luggage tracker only works if users maintain it. In addition, travelers also log all of their preferences into the app which radically improves the accuracy of the recommendations.
**Test Method:** Prototype the luggage tracker and trip re-optimization as standalone features. Run a 2-week beta with 20 multi-destination travelers. Measure daily active logging rate and whether users complete their trip without reporting a lost item or able to complete all of their proposed activities within the prescribed time.
**Success Metric:** 70% of beta users log items at least once per location change, and at least 3 users report the tracker prevented a forgotten item.
